I’m a specialized generalist who uses code as a tool to bring designs to life. With a background ranging from print design to user experience to front-end development, I do my best work at the intersection of brand, product, and engineering.

I’m currently Brand Lead at Braintrust, a platform providing tools for agent observability. I’ve worked in a wide range of design roles over the years, across tech companies and independent studios, including:

  • Staff Product Designer & Design Engineer at Shopify, where I built and maintained the design system for the App and Theme Stores, bridging the gap between our UX and engineering teams while shipping new features to production.
  • Senior Designer at GitHub, where I was part of the team that designed, built, and maintained marketing sites for the world’s largest open source platform.
  • Design Lead at Upstatement, where I led a group called Release Team that focused on long-term product evolution for clients with limited in-house teams.

If you’d like to chat about hiking, gardening, neighborhood bears, the finer points of homemade pierogi, or (if you insist) all things design and development, don’t hesitate to say hi or send me a note on LinkedIn.
